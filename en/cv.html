Research projects

NCN · OPUS 27 · 2024/53/B/NZ6/02963 · 2025–2028

The “Trojan horse” mechanism used by Candida albicans protease Sap6 in evading the neutrophil response

Principal Investigator, budget PLN 1,507,761. We ask whether the mechanism affects all neutrophils or particular subpopulations, which endocytic routes bring Sap6 into the cell, where it goes and what else it degrades — and whether fungal extracellular vesicles act as an alternative carrier.
